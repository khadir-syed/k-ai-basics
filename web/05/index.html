Why AI forgets

A notepad that only fits so much

Imagine telling a friend a long story, but your friend has a tiny notepad that only fits a few lines. Every time the notepad is full and you say something new, your friend rubs out the oldest line to make room. Later, if you ask "what did I say at the start?" — it's gone. Your friend didn't choose to forget. The notepad was just full.

AI chatbots work the same way. They can only "see" a certain number of tokens at once — the little pieces words are cut into (see Demo 01). That limit is called the context window. When a chat gets too long, the oldest messages fall off the notepad.

How are tokens counted? Each message is cut into tokens exactly the way GPT-2 does it in Demo 01, and the pieces are counted. For example, "Hi! My name is Priya." is 8 tokens:

  1. Hi17250
  2. !0
  3. ·My2011
  4. ·name1438
  5. ·is318
  6. ·Pri4389
  7. ya3972
  8. .13

Roughly: a common word is 1 token, and punctuation like ! and . counts too. A name GPT-2 rarely saw, like Priya, gets cut into smaller pieces — "·Pri" + "ya" — and other languages and emoji take even more. (· marks a space, and the small number is each token's ID.)

This page gives the AI a tiny notepad on purpose — just 30 tokens — so you can watch it fill up and see exactly which message gets rubbed out.

Watch the notepad fill up

4 messages, one after another, with a notepad of 30 tokens. The bar shows how full it is after each one.

  1. You: Hi! My name is Priya. +8 tokens

    8/30 8/30 tokens

  2. You: I live in Hyderabad with my cat. +9 tokens

    17/30 17/30 tokens

  3. You: My favourite food is chicken biryani. +9 tokens

    26/30 26/30 tokens

  4. You: What is my name? +5 tokens

    ✗ Rubbed out: Hi! My name is Priya. (8 tokens)

    23/30 23/30 tokens

What the AI can still see now:

  • I live in Hyderabad with my cat. (9 tokens)
  • My favourite food is chicken biryani. (9 tokens)
  • What is my name? (5 tokens)

How is a number like "+8 tokens" worked out? See how tokens are counted, at the top.

❌ At the very moment you ask "What is my name?", the message with your name is the one that falls off. The AI isn't being rude — it simply can't see that message any more.

The lesson: an AI only remembers what still fits in its context window — always losing the oldest part first. Today's AIs have huge notepads, hundreds of thousands of tokens, so this happens much later. But it still happens in very long chats, which is why an AI can lose track of something you said early on.
