AI basics, in your browser

Small, hands-on demos that show how AI really works — one idea at a time. Open one and try it: nothing to install, no sign-up, and nothing you type leaves your device.

The first demo you open downloads a small helper (about 13 MB) so Python can run inside your browser. After that it's quick. (Demos 02 and 06 need a bigger download, and ask before it starts.)

Demo 01 How AI reads words AI cuts your words into small pieces called tokens, then guesses the next one. Split your own sentence and see GPT-2's guesses. Demo 02 How AI looks things up Before AI answers from your documents, it finds the paragraphs that match your question. See when it picks the right one — and when it doesn't. Demo 03 Watch an AI agent think An agent decides which tool to use before it answers. Watch every step — including when it picks the wrong one. Demo 04 Who should handle this? An AI system picks which helper should handle your request. See how it scores each one — and when it sends you to the wrong one. Demo 05 Why AI forgets An AI can only keep so many tokens in mind. Chat with a tiny notepad and watch the oldest messages get rubbed out. Demo 06 When AI makes things up A real AI, asked about a made-up town, invents names and dates with total confidence. See what changes when it looks things up first. Demo 07 Ask AI better The same AI gives a far better answer when you ask more clearly. See 4 real examples, then check your own question. Demo 08 Hidden orders in documents A hidden sentence in a document can take over an AI agent. Watch a filter catch it — and then miss it. Demo 09 Hide private info from AI A "black marker" hides private details before an AI sees them. See what it catches — and what slips through. Demo 10 Two agents, one job Two AI agents pass a bug report between them. Watch each step of the handoff — and where it can go wrong.
