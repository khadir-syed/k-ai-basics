Every October, Puddlewick holds the Great Puddle Jump Festival. Children and
grown-ups take turns jumping into the biggest puddle in the market square,
and three judges score each jump for the size of its splash.

The festival winner gets the Golden Welly, a single golden rain boot that is
kept in the town hall until the next festival. Last year the Golden Welly was
won by a nine-year-old girl named Tilly Marsh, whose splash soaked the mayor.

The festival ends with a lantern parade along the harbour. Every family makes
a lantern shaped like a fish, and 347 fish lanterns were counted in the most
recent parade.
