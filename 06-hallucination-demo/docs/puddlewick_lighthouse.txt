Puddlewick Lighthouse stands on a rock at the edge of the harbour. The
lighthouse was built in 1887 and painted with red and white stripes, so ships
can spot it even in heavy rain.

The lighthouse keeper is Mr. Oswin Fairweather, who has climbed its 142 steps
every evening for thirty years to check the lamp. He says he has never once
missed a day.

The lighthouse light flashes three times and then pauses. That pattern tells
sailors they are near Puddlewick, and the people of the town call it "the
three winks."
