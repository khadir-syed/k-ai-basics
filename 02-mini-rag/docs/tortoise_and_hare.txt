A hare once laughed at a tortoise for being so slow. The tortoise smiled and
challenged the hare to a race, and the hare, sure of an easy win, agreed at
once.

The hare shot off the starting line in a burst of speed, far ahead of the
slow, steady tortoise. Confident he had the race won, the hare decided to lie
down under a shady tree and take a nap before the finish line.

While the hare slept, the tortoise kept walking, one slow step after another,
never stopping and never resting. Step by step, the tortoise crawled closer
and closer to the finish line.

The hare woke up with a start and dashed toward the finish line as fast as he
could run. But it was too late. The tortoise had already crossed the line,
winning the race through patience and persistence rather than speed.

The moral of the story is simple: slow and steady wins the race.
