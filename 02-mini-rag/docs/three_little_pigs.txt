Once there were three little pigs who each decided to build their own house.
The first pig was lazy and built his house out of straw, finishing in a
single afternoon so he could go play.

The second pig worked a little harder and built his house out of sticks. It
took longer than the straw house, but the second pig was still eager to
finish quickly and relax.

The third pig worked slowly and carefully, building his house out of heavy
bricks. His brothers teased him for taking so long, but the third pig knew
that a strong house needed a strong foundation.

One day a hungry wolf came prowling through the woods and found the house of
straw. The wolf huffed and puffed and blew the straw house down with ease,
and the first pig ran to his brother's stick house in fear.

The wolf followed and huffed and puffed again, blowing the stick house down
just as easily. Both pigs ran together to the third pig's brick house and
locked the door behind them.

The wolf huffed and puffed with all his might, but the brick house did not
budge. No matter how hard he tried, the wolf could not blow the sturdy brick
house down, and the three pigs stayed safe inside.
