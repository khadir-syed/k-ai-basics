All through the warm summer, a hardworking ant gathered grains of food and
carried them back to her nest, storing them away one trip at a time for the
winter ahead.

Nearby, a grasshopper spent his summer days singing and playing music in the
sun, mocking the ant for working so hard instead of enjoying the warm
weather while it lasted.

The ant kept gathering food every single day, ignoring the grasshopper's
teasing, because she knew that summer would not last forever and winter would
eventually come.

When winter finally arrived, the ground froze and snow covered the fields.
The grasshopper had no food stored away and grew hungry and cold, with no
music left to play.

The grasshopper went to the ant's nest and asked for something to eat. The
ant welcomed him in and shared her stored food, reminding him gently that a
little hard work in good times prepares you for hard times later.
